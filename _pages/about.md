I am a PhD student in the Department of Computer Science and Information Engineering at National Taiwan University and a recipient of the 2024 National Science and Technology Council Graduate Research Fellowship. I am currently advised by Prof. Tyng-Lu Liu at the CVML Lab at Academia Sinica and Prof. Yung-Yu Chuang at the CMLab at the Department of Computer Science and Information Engineering at National Taiwan University, and my research focuses on synthetic image detection, information bottleneck methods, and 3D reconstruction and generation. I work with the Computer Vision and Machine Learning Lab at Academia Sinica and have also conducted research in the Communication and Multimedia Lab at NTU, including industry-academia projects with ASUS on semantic segmentation and image denoising. My work has led to publications including "HSIC Bottleneck for Cross-Generator and Domain-Incremental Synthetic Image Detection," accepted to ICLR 2026, and "Disparity-Guided Light Field Video Synthesis with Temporal Consistency," presented at MIPR 2022. I have also worked as a Research Intern at Microsoft Taiwan, where I studied aggregation algorithms for zero-shot face anti-spoofing with linguistic model features and evaluated anti-spoofing systems through penetration testing.
