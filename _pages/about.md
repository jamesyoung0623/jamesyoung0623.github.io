I am a PhD student in the Department of Computer Science and Information Engineering at National Taiwan University and a recipient of the 2024 National Science and Technology Council Graduate Research Fellowship. I am currently advised by [Prof. Tyng-Lu Liu](https://homepage.iis.sinica.edu.tw/pages/liutyng/index_en.html) at the CVML Lab, Academia Sinica, and [Prof. Yung-Yu Chuang](https://www.csie.ntu.edu.tw/~cyy/) at the CMLab, National Taiwan University. My research focuses on synthetic image detection, information bottleneck methods, and 3D reconstruction and generation. I have also worked as a Research Intern at Microsoft Taiwan, where I studied aggregation algorithms for zero-shot face anti-spoofing with linguistic model features and evaluated anti-spoofing systems through penetration testing.
